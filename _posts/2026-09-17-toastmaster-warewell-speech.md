These are highlights of my thrid English speech.

>The odds of a dream happening is really low. But, if you never try, you never know.

>Not every "stepping out of your comfort zone" story has a happy ending. Sometimes, you take the leap and fall. 

>However, I believe that taking that leap will always bring you growth. Sometimes, the reward is simply becoming a braver and stronger you.

>Whether you decide to stay and enjoy what you have built over the years, choose to leave a "discomfort zone" to find your next comfort zone, or simply want to step out of your comfort zone for a new adventure... As long as you know what you truly want and follow your heart, these are all incredible choices.

>Believe in yourself. Choose what you love, and love what you choose. I wish us all a very happy life.
